Emberwatch — you are the Keeper of the Last Ember. Shades crawl out of the dark to snuff its flame: hold the line for 10 waves across a moonlit glade. Move with WASD or arrows, aim with the mouse, hold click or Space to fire ember bolts, Shift to dash. Slain shades drop shards that feed the flame; clear a wave to choose one of three blessings (faster fire, twin flame, piercing light, and more). Win by surviving all ten waves, including the Gloom Herald on wave 10. Lose if your health — or the Ember's flame — reaches zero. A complete original 2D canvas game: title, HUD, upgrade choices, pause, victory and game-over screens, synthesized WebAudio sound, no network requests, no build step.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and press Space to begin.
